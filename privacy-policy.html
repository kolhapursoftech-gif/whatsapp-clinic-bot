Privacy Policy

This WhatsApp appointment-booking assistant is a service provided for Test Clinic.

What we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp to book an appointment, we collect your name, age, preferred appointment date, and WhatsApp phone number. This information is used only to schedule and manage your appointment.

How we use it

Your information is stored securely in a private Google Sheet accessible only to clinic staff, and is used solely for appointment scheduling and to contact you about your appointment.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except as required to operate this service (WhatsApp/Meta, for message delivery).

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, please contact the clinic directly.
